Our Story

A Legacy Built on
Excellence & Care

Rajarajeshwari Dental College & Hospital was founded in 1994 with a singular vision — to establish a centre of excellence in dental education and healthcare in Karnataka. Named after the revered goddess Rajarajeshwari, the institution was built on the principles of compassion, integrity, and academic rigour.

Affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) and approved by the Dental Council of India (DCI), the college has grown from a single BDS batch to one of Karnataka's most recognised dental institutions — offering BDS, MDS across nine specialties, and active research programmes.

Today, the hospital manages over 50,000 patient visits annually, supported by state-of-the-art dental operatories, a digital radiology unit, simulation labs, and a 24×7 dental emergency facility.
