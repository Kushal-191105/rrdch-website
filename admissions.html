Admission Criteria

Eligibility Criteria

🦷

BDS — Undergraduate

Bachelor of Dental Surgery

📚
Academic Qualification 10+2 or equivalent with PCB Physics, Chemistry & Biology as compulsory subjects
📊
Minimum Marks 50% aggregate in PCB (General) 40% for SC / ST / OBC candidates as per DCI norms
🎂
Age Requirement Minimum 17 years as on 31st December 2026 No upper age limit as per Supreme Court ruling
📝
Entrance Exam NEET-UG (Mandatory) Valid NEET-UG score is compulsory for all candidates
🏛
Admission Route KEA Counselling + Management Quota Government seats through KEA; Management seats via RRDCH
🎓

MDS — Postgraduate

Master of Dental Surgery

📚
Academic Qualification BDS degree from a DCI recognised university Must have completed 1-year compulsory internship
📊
Minimum Marks 55% aggregate in BDS (General) 50% for SC / ST / OBC candidates
📝
Entrance Exam NEET-MDS (Mandatory) Valid NEET-MDS score required for all candidates
🏛
Admission Route KEA PG Counselling + Management Quota Stray vacancy rounds as per RGUHS schedule
⚠️

NEET is Mandatory for All Dental Admissions in India

As per the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India and the Dental Council of India, a valid NEET score is compulsory for admission to BDS and MDS programmes across all dental colleges in India, including RRDCH. Candidates without a valid NEET score will not be considered for admission.

Fee Payment Notes

Fees are payable annually at the start of each academic year.
Demand draft or online NEFT/IMPS transfer accepted. Cash not accepted above ₹2,000.
Government quota fees are regulated by the Karnataka Fee Regulatory Committee (KFRC).
NRI quota fees are payable in USD equivalent — contact admissions for current rate.
One-time refundable caution deposit of ₹25,000 applicable for all students.
Fees shown are indicative. Final fees as per KFRC order applicable each year.

🎓 Scholarships & Fee Concessions

SC / ST / OBC students may apply for state government scholarships through the Karnataka Scholarship Portal. Merit-based fee waivers are available for top-ranking NEET students admitted under management quota — contact the admissions office for eligibility details.
